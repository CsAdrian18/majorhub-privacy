# MajorHub Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026

MajorHub is built for students and families researching college majors, university costs, and career outcomes. This Privacy Policy explains our practices in plain language.

## 1. No Personal Data Collected & No Accounts

MajorHub has **no user accounts**, no sign-up or sign-in screens, and collects **zero personal data**. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location.

## 2. Saved Items Stay On-Device Only

Everything you save or customize in MajorHub stays locally on your phone using on-device storage (`AsyncStorage`):

- Your saved majors and saved schools shortlist
- Your active side-by-side major and school comparisons
- Your Major-Fit Quiz answers and match results
- Your selected theme, tuition residency preference (In-State vs. Out-of-State), and custom scoring priority weights
- Cached public catalog data for offline browsing

None of this information is ever sent to our servers. You can clear all locally stored items at any time in the app under **Settings → Reset Local App Data & Cache**, or by uninstalling the app.

## 3. Read-Only Public Catalog Data

When your device is online, MajorHub makes read-only requests to fetch official public statistics from U.S. government datasets (U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard, NCES IPEDS, Bureau of Labor Statistics, O*NET, BEA Regional Price Parities, and OPE Campus Safety). These requests do not include any personal information, account tokens, or user identifiers.

## 4. No Ads, No Tracking, and No Data Selling

- **No Ads:** MajorHub contains no third-party advertising SDKs.
- **No Tracking:** We do not track you across apps or websites and never request iOS App Tracking Transparency (IDFA) permissions.
- **No Selling or Sharing Data:** Because we collect no personal data, we never sell, rent, or share student information with colleges, recruiters, data brokers, or student loan marketers.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Because MajorHub has no accounts and collects no personal information from users of any age, the app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
